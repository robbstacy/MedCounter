## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Never run out of an important medication again.

Med Counter keeps track of how many pills you have left, when each medication will run out, and when you need to call the pharmacy — for yourself and for the people you care for.

WHY MED COUNTER

Managing medications for a family member means juggling a dozen bottles, different schedules, and refill lead times. Med Counter does the math for you: enter what you have, and it tells you exactly when each medication runs out and when to reorder.

FEATURES

• Multiple profiles — track medications for several people from one device
• Flexible schedules — once daily, twice daily, three times daily, or as-needed, with morning / midday / bedtime / custom dose times
• Automatic supply math — days remaining, run-out date, and a reorder-by date based on your pharmacy's lead time
• Restock log — record every refill with date, quantity, and notes
• Full audit trail — every change is recorded, so counts always add up
• Refill and dose reminders — local notifications, generated on your device
• Pharmacy details — store pharmacy, Rx number, and refills remaining per medication
• Export everything — one tap creates a readable summary plus CSV files you can share, print, or archive

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your medication list is nobody's business but yours.

• No account, no sign-up
• No internet connection required or used
• No cloud, no sync, no servers
• No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs
• All data stays in a local database on your device
• Export and share only what you choose, when you choose

SIMPLE ON PURPOSE

Med Counter focuses on one job — making sure you never run out — and does it well. Add a medication in seconds, restock in two taps, and see everything that needs attention at a glance.

NOTE

Med Counter is an organizational tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always follow the instructions of your doctor and pharmacist.
```
