Privacy Policy

App: Med Counter
Developer: Robert Stacy
Effective date: June 9, 2026

Summary

Med Counter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Med Counter stores medication information (names, dosing schedules, quantities, pharmacy details) and usage history exclusively in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device. We have no servers, no cloud sync, and no analytics.

Data we share

None. We do not share data with third parties, advertisers, or anyone else. There are no third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics frameworks in this app.

Internet access

Med Counter does not require or use an internet connection. All functionality is local-only. The only network interaction occurs when you tap "Privacy Policy" to open this page in a browser, which is initiated by you and handled entirely by your browser.

Notifications

Med Counter may send local notifications (refill reminders, dose reminders) if you enable them. These are generated on your device by the Android system and are never transmitted anywhere.

Data export

The "Export all data" feature writes files to your device's temporary cache and shows the Android share sheet, allowing you to send those files to apps of your choice (email, cloud storage, etc.). We have no involvement in or access to where you choose to share those files.

Health information

Med Counter is an inventory-tracking tool. Any medication information you enter is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

Children

Med Counter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
